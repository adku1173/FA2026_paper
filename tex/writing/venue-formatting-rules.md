# FA2026 venue formatting rules

Source: [FA2026 author instructions](https://fa2026.eu)

## Document

- Class: `\documentclass[peer-review]{fa2026}` or `[non-peer-review]`
- A4, 2-column, fully justified; 4–6 pages including references; max 25 MB PDF
- Language: English, original, unpublished

## Layout

- Body: 10 pt Latin Modern Roman, 0.95 line spacing
- Abstract: max 200 words
- Keywords: 3–5 terms
- Figure captions below, table captions above
- Equations on separate lines with right-aligned numbers

## References

- IEEE-style numbered citations with `\cite{}`
- Use `biblatex` with `biber`

## Restrictions

- Do not modify headers, footers, or page numbers
- PDF must not be encrypted or password protected
